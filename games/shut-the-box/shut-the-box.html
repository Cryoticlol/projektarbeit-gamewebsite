Shut the Box

How to play

Rules

  • Start with tiles 1 to 9 open.
  • Roll both dice and add their values.
  • Select one or more open tiles whose numbers add up to your roll. Use each tile at most once per turn.
  • Confirm your selection to close those tiles for the rest of the game. Then roll again.
  • You lose if no combination of open tiles adds up to your roll.
  • You win when all nine tiles are closed.
