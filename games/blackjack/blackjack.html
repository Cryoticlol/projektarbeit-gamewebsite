Blackjack Rules

  • Get as close to 21 as possible without going over.
  • Number cards count as their face value.
  • Jack, Queen and King count as 10.
  • Aces count as 11 or 1, whichever is better for your hand.
  • The dealer must draw until reaching at least 17.
  • A regular win pays 1:1.
  • A natural Blackjack (21 with the first two cards) pays 2:1 profit.
  • A push (draw) returns your bet.
  • Hit draws another card, you can hit multiple times until you stand or bust (go over 21).
  • Stand ends your turn.
  • Double doubles your bet and gives you exactly one more card.
  • Split lets you play two hands when your first two cards have the same rank.
